## Procedure

1. Treat a coordinator's or specialist's output as a proposal, never proof of its own correctness or authority.
2. Require independently checked review evidence before the review-required transition. A client flag or model assertion is not an authentication mechanism.
3. Check source references, versions, matter scope, unsupported claims, missing evidence, adverse material, and uncertainty. Do not infer deadlines or legal conclusions from receipt metadata.
4. Preserve attorney control of legal decisions, conflict clearance, engagement, filing, and external communications.
5. PR1 uses deterministic synthetic review fixtures. Do not add an ADK reviewer, model invocation, live research adapter, or production evidence verifier as part of a control repair.
6. Exercise missing/false review checks and confirm denial causes no state or audit mutation. Record the limits of what the synthetic gate proves.
